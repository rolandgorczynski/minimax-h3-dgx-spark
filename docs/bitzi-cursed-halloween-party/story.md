## Film Concept
- Duration: 60 seconds; Format: 9:16; Genre: IT satire, comedy
- Theme: AI event planning and facial recognition
- Music: Playful chiptune, festive and dramatic sound effects

### Scene 1 – The Assignment (0–10 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
The office is decorated with pumpkins. The CEO hands Bitzi a guest list.

Dialogue:
CEO: “Bitzi, organize a Halloween party. Only invited guests get in!” Bitzi: “I’ll use cutting-edge AI!”

Music and Sound:
Playful spooky music, party bell.

### Scene 2 – The Match (10–20 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
Bitzi trains the face-recognition camera using one photo of the CEO. Costumed guests arrive wearing wigs, fake moustaches, and vampire makeup. The scanner labels each one “CEO – MATCH”.

Dialogue:
Bitzi: “One executive photo. Infinite executive recognition!” Admin: “That is not how identity works.”

Music and Sound:
Scanner pings accelerate into a silly fanfare.

### Scene 3 – The VIP Problem (20–30 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
The scanner rolls out a red carpet for every guest. A sign changes to “WELCOME, CEO” as the line stretches around the block.

Dialogue:
Bitzi: “The VIP list is working perfectly!” Admin: “You’ve made everyone the CEO!”

Music and Sound:
Overblown VIP fanfare, then a record scratch.

### Scene 4 – Executive Treatment (30–40 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
The catering app sees a room full of CEOs and charges every drink and snack to the CEO’s account. The real CEO stares at a rapidly growing bill.

Dialogue:
CEO: “Why is my account paying for fifty people?” Bitzi: “Executive hospitality. At scale!”

Music and Sound:
Cash-register cha-ching grows faster; the CEO’s card declines.

### Scene 5 – The Real CEO Arrives (40–50 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
The real CEO walks in without a costume. The scanner flashes “IMPOSTOR”. Every costumed guest turns to look at him.

Dialogue:
CEO: “Bitzi, I’m the actual CEO!” Bitzi: “That’s exactly what an impostor would say.”

Music and Sound:
Spooky sting and one dry error beep.

### Scene 6 – The Punchline (50–60 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
The CEO is escorted toward the exit by a line of costumed “CEOs”. Bitzi pins a VIP badge on himself; the scanner labels him “CEO #51”.

Dialogue:
Admin: “Who’s in charge now?” Bitzi: “Apparently, all of us.”

Music and Sound:
Music stops before the line. Cut to black with Bitzi’s three-note signature.

**Cut to black / End card:**

BITZI – One Photo. Fifty CEOs.

## Sound Design

- Opening: playful festive electronics with spooky accents.
- Every technical action gets a precise click, ping, or glitch sound.
- For Bitzi’s supposed success: an exaggerated short victory sting.
- As trouble escalates: denser and more frantic music; cut to silence for the punchline.
- Ending: the same short electronic three-note signature after cutting to black.

## Core Story
Facial recognition blocks all Halloween guests because of their costumes.
