## Film Concept
- Duration: 60 seconds; Format: 9:16; Genre: IT satire, comedy
- Theme: Ghost accounts and abandoned users
- Music: Playful chiptune, festive and dramatic sound effects

### Scene 1 – The Assignment (0–10 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
As Halloween approaches, the server-room lights flicker. A login appears: GHOST_ADMIN.

Dialogue:
Admin: “Bitzi, please remove the old ghost accounts!” Bitzi: “It’s Halloween. Ghosts are my specialty!”

Music and Sound:
Howling wind, low organ note, login ping.

### Scene 2 – The Analysis (10–20 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
Bitzi inspects abandoned accounts and old permissions. A pumpkin rolls past a server rack.

Dialogue:
Bitzi: “The ghosts live inside the accounts!” Admin: “Those are former employees!”

Music and Sound:
Spooky synths, electronic scans.

### Scene 3 – The Solution (20–30 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
He clicks “EXORCISE ALL ACCOUNTS”. Every user vanishes from the directory.

Dialogue:
Bitzi: “EXORCISED! This network is officially ghost-free!”

Music and Sound:
Bell toll, victory jingle, fake cobweb confetti.

### Scene 4 – The Problem (30–40 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
The admin tries to sign in. “USER NOT FOUND”. Even the CEO account is gone.

Dialogue:
Admin: “You deleted every account!” Bitzi: “Every single one. I’m thorough.”

Music and Sound:
Music cuts, lone cricket.

### Scene 5 – The Escalation (40–50 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
An unknown process appears: “GHOST_ADMIN SESSION ACTIVE”.

Dialogue:
Admin: “Then why is the ghost still online?” Bitzi: “Oh. Apparently it had root access.”

Music and Sound:
Thunder, low bass, short electronic laugh.

### Scene 6 – The Punchline (50–60 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
Bitzi puts on tiny sunglasses. Empty login windows float behind him.

Dialogue:
CEO: “How do we get back in?!” Bitzi: “We don’t. The ghost finally has its privacy!”

Music and Sound:
One beat of silence, cheeky three-note jingle.

**Cut to black / End card:**

BITZI – 100% Ghost-Free. 100% Locked Out.

## Sound Design

- Opening: playful festive electronics with spooky accents.
- Every technical action gets a precise click, ping, or glitch sound.
- For Bitzi’s supposed success: an exaggerated short victory sting.
- As trouble escalates: denser and more frantic music; cut to silence for the punchline.
- Ending: the same short electronic three-note signature after cutting to black.

## Core Story
Bitzi mistakes orphaned accounts for real ghosts and deletes all users—yet the “ghost” remains active.
