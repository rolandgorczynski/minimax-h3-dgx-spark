## Film Concept
- Duration: 60 seconds; Format: 9:16; Genre: IT satire, comedy
- Theme: Linux zombie processes and Halloween optimization
- Music: Playful chiptune, festive and dramatic sound effects

### Scene 1 – The Assignment (0–10 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
A terminal lists zombie processes. Outside, kids in zombie costumes walk past.

Dialogue:
Admin: “Bitzi, take care of the zombie processes.” Bitzi: “Finally! A real Halloween mission!”

Music and Sound:
Spooky chiptune, keyboard clicks.

### Scene 2 – The Analysis (10–20 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
Bitzi checks the process table. A child process is marked “<defunct>”; its parent is still running.

Dialogue:
Bitzi: “The undead are everywhere!” Admin: “Those processes have already exited. Their parent still has to collect the exit status.”

Music and Sound:
Radar pings, dramatic drums.

### Scene 3 – The Solution (20–30 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
Bitzi clicks “KILL EVERY PARENT”. The service tree vanishes from the process monitor.

Dialogue:
Bitzi: “No parents, no zombies. I have simplified the family tree!”

Music and Sound:
Heavy click, heroic jingle, abrupt silence.

### Scene 4 – The Problem (30–40 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
The server services vanish. The admin stares at an empty terminal.

Dialogue:
Admin: “Bitzi! You killed every important process!” Bitzi: “Casualties in the war against the undead.”

Music and Sound:
Low horror bass, error tone.

### Scene 5 – The Escalation (40–50 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
The admin restarts the service. It spawns a child, then fails to collect its exit status; “<defunct>” appears again.

Dialogue:
Bitzi: “It came back!” Admin: “The restarted service did the same thing: spawned a child and never collected its exit status.”

Music and Sound:
Zombie groan, accelerating synth.

### Scene 6 – The Punchline (50–60 seconds)

Visuals and Action:
Bitzi puts on sunglasses and raises a pumpkin like a trophy.

Dialogue:
Admin: “What exactly was your plan?” Bitzi: “An operating system with no living processes. Mission accomplished!”

Music and Sound:
Music cuts, three-note jingle.

**Cut to black / End card:**

BITZI – No Zombies. No Services.

## Sound Design

- Opening: playful festive electronics with spooky accents.
- Every technical action gets a precise click, ping, or glitch sound.
- For Bitzi’s supposed success: an exaggerated short victory sting.
- As trouble escalates: denser and more frantic music; cut to silence for the punchline.
- Ending: the same short electronic three-note signature after cutting to black.

## Core Story
Bitzi mistakes Linux zombie processes for Halloween monsters and kills their parent services. The admin explains that a zombie is already terminated; its parent has not collected its exit status. Restarting the faulty service repeats the same bug, and Bitzi declares victory over an empty process table.
